# Geisha Gains — Banner & Logo Design Prompt

## 🎯 Design Style: Minimalistic Brutalism

**Core Aesthetic:**
- Dark brutalist design with stark contrasts
- Monospace typography (JetBrains Mono preferred)
- Primary colors: #121212 (background), #FFFFFF (text/borders), #FF0000 (accents)
- Thick borders (4px minimum) with sharp 90-degree angles
- No gradients, shadows, or rounded corners
- Raw, unpolished, industrial feel

- Error: #EF4444 (Red-500)

## 📝 Typography Guidelines

**Fonts:**
- Primary: JetBrains Mono (monospace, technical feel)
- Weights: Regular, Bold, Black
- Sizes: Use geometric scaling (24pt, 36pt, 48pt, 72pt)

**Text Hierarchy:**
- H1: 72pt Bold - Main titles
- H2: 48pt Bold - Subtitles
- H3: 36pt Regular - Section headers
- Body: 24pt Regular - Main content
- Small: 18pt Regular - Metadata/captions

## 🖼️ Visual Metaphors to Include

1. **War Room Theme**: Military command center aesthetic
2. **Intelligence**: Radar screens, data visualizations
3. **Crypto Trading**: Price charts, market data
4. **AI Analysis**: Circuit patterns, neural network motifs
5. **Coffee Driven**: Coffee stains, steam effects (subtle)

## 🚫 Design Restrictions

- **NO gradients or shadows**
- **NO rounded corners** (sharp 90-degree angles only)
- **NO organic shapes** (geometric only)
- **NO pastel colors** (high contrast only)
- **NO serif fonts** (monospace only)
- **NO illustrations** (icons and geometric shapes only)
